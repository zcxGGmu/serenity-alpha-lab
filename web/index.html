Operating loop

Research starts with data lineage, not a model answer.

Every stage either produces a deterministic artifact, a cited research claim, or a visible gate decision. The homepage mirrors that contract instead of hiding it behind generic AI marketing language.

Trust boundaries

AI is inside the evidence box.

Serenity Alpha Lab separates deterministic financial computation from language-model synthesis. Reports may explain a thesis, but they cannot silently recalculate returns, bypass source trust, or relabel legacy signal evaluation as formal portfolio backtesting.

  • ExecutionNo live trading or automatic broker execution.
  • RuntimeNo Provider or LLM runtime from document, renderer, validator, grant, or config contracts.
  • InputNo unsafe URL, upload, HTML, or report-link path through the presentation boundary.
  • ReleaseNo release-ready claim until Gate G6 passes.
