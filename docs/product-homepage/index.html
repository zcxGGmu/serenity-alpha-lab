Trust boundaries

AI is inside the evidence box.

Serenity Alpha Lab separates deterministic financial computation from language-model synthesis. Reports may explain a thesis, but they cannot silently recalculate returns, bypass source trust, or relabel legacy signal evaluation as formal portfolio backtesting.

  • No live trading or automatic broker execution.
  • No Provider or LLM runtime from document, renderer, validator, grant, or config contracts.
  • No unsafe URL, upload, HTML, or report-link path through the presentation boundary.
  • No release-ready claim until Gate G6 passes.
